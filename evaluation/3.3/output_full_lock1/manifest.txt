== Design
gap lengths: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 25, 30, 40, 60] (40 and 60 are EXTRAPOLATION beyond the longest real gap, 30)
context required observed on each side: 10 frames, all 12 body joints observed over the span and context
starts: first start frame 10, then every 25 frames; every body joint hidden one at a time
batching: one hidden gap per joint per fill_gaps call, at least 60 frames of observed data between spans
methods: ['hold', 'spline', 'kalman_smooth', 'kalman_smooth_noclamp', 'kalman_smooth_mirror', 'kalman_causal', 'kalman_causal_noclamp', 'trunk_offset']
OcclusionParams per method: {'hold': None, 'spline': {'force_method': 'spline'}, 'kalman_smooth': {'force_method': 'kalman', 'tier2_clamp': True, 'tier2_mirror': False, 'tier2_causal': False}, 'kalman_smooth_noclamp': {'force_method': 'kalman', 'tier2_clamp': False, 'tier2_mirror': False, 'tier2_causal': False}, 'kalman_smooth_mirror': {'force_method': 'kalman', 'tier2_clamp': True, 'tier2_mirror': True, 'tier2_causal': False}, 'kalman_causal': {'force_method': 'kalman', 'tier2_clamp': True, 'tier2_mirror': False, 'tier2_causal': True}, 'kalman_causal_noclamp': {'force_method': 'kalman', 'tier2_clamp': False, 'tier2_mirror': False, 'tier2_causal': True}, 'trunk_offset': {'force_method': 'trunk_offset'}}
clamp on/off is reported for limb joints and mirror on/off for trunk joints (the other switch has no effect on them; tested)
error: Euclidean px, per trial the mean over the hidden frames; mid = mean over the central frame(s); torso-normalised columns divide by the clip's MEDIAN torso length (shoulder midpoint to hip midpoint, frames with the four trunk joints observed)
smooth/sudden: peak second difference of the TRUE position over the hidden span plus 2 frames each side / the clip's median torso length; terciles WITHIN each (gap length, joint), pooled over clips; lowest third smooth, top third sudden, middle unlabelled
cut points: cutpoints.csv, written at 2026-11-25T10:09:23 (run started 2026-11-25T10:09:23), before any fill was run; the rule was fixed in this file before the full run; development smoke runs looked at errors for plumbing only and the rule was not changed afterwards
crossover bootstrap: 1000 resamples of the 5 clips with replacement, seed 0; descriptive only, not a significance test
