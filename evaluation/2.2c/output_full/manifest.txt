== Definitions
shared frames: candidate and F both defined. torso length: shoulder midpoint -> hip midpoint per frame.
coverage: defined frames / all frames. defined-to-None: frames defined at t-1 and not at t, per 1,000 frames. defined runs: maximal runs of consecutive defined frames.
jitter J(t) = ||p(t+1) - 2 p(t) + p(t-1)|| / L(t), torso lengths per frame^2, for each t where p(t-1), p(t), p(t+1) and L(t) are defined; no division by dt^2; cannot separate estimator noise from real acceleration. common support: all candidates defined.
percentile: linear interpolation between order statistics.
E limb gate: all of a segment's joints have a position and raw confidence >= cut-off (hand, foot: their single joint); trunk joints keep the D-007 0.5 rule.
