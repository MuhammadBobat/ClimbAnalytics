== What was run
1. cache_boxes.py: YOLOv8n person boxes at conf >= 0.05 for every frame of the five clips (validation/box_cache/).
2. replay_check.py: the legacy rule on the cache reproduced validation/output_full (box columns and n_person_boxes) on all 5,886 frames, 0 mismatches (replay_check.txt).
3. sweep_lock_rule.py: full factorial of the rule family on the cache; hard filters (i) IMG_2419 592-614 has no wrong frame against the author-CONFIRMED reference track (reference_track_2419.csv), (ii) IMG_2417 identical to legacy, (iii) first 100 frames unchanged on every frame the legacy rule tracked (the literal 'identical' count is also reported).
4. simulate_gaps.py: climber hidden in place in real cached boxes for L in 5, 10, 20, 40, 80 frames, trial starts every 50 frames from 20, margin 10 frames, hide IoU 0.3, re-acquisition IoU 0.5. LIMITATION: the climber never reappears elsewhere; the clips contain no real gap longer than 8 frames.
5. select_setting.py: the author's selection order (stated 2026-11-03, AFTER the stage-2 results were seen; NOT pre-registered): a) hard filters; b) zero accepted non-climber boxes at every hide length and zero 2419 wrong frames; c) most correct 2419-window frames, then highest re-acquisition at 80 frames; d) largest margin, then fewest components. Tie-break between K = 1 and K = 10 (mine, confirmed by the author as K = 10): higher total re-acquisition.
6. make_lists_and_sheets.py, reference_track_sheet.py: review lists and contact sheets (sheets gitignored).
7. bytetrack_reference.py: one untuned ByteTrack run (reference only).
