== Analyses (definitions; no interpretation in the files)
offset: per frame the difference vector (segment minus proxy) is projected on the torso axis (shoulder midpoint -> hip midpoint); offset_along_torso_axis_torso is its mean over the clip in torso lengths (positive = toward the hips); timevarying_* is |d - offset*axis| / torso length per frame (anything across the torso axis is part of it). ALL = frame-weighted mean offset, per-clip offsets removed before pooling.
speed correlation: Pearson r between proxy speed and segment-model speed over steps where both are defined (empty if fewer than 3 steps or no variance).
top_speed_differences.csv: the 10 largest |segment speed - proxy speed| steps pooled over clips (primary sex); the 3 lowest-confidence joints (of the 14 used) at each frame of the step.

== Models
baseline: trunk-centroid proxy, equal weights on shoulders and hips (D-005 follow-up)
segment model: de Leva (1996), J Biomech 29(9):1223-1230, Table 4 (p. 1228)
table rows used: head VERT-MIDG (mass only, at ear midpoint); trunk MIDS-MIDH; upper arm SJC-EJC; forearm EJC-WJC; hand WJC-MET3 (mass only, at wrist); thigh HJC-KJC; shank KJC-AJC (alternative-endpoints row); foot HEEL-TTIP (mass only, at ankle)
required joints (14): left_ankle, left_ear, left_elbow, left_hip, left_knee, left_shoulder, left_wrist, right_ankle, right_ear, right_elbow, right_hip, right_knee, right_shoulder, right_wrist
sex tables: primary = male; sensitivity = female, mean (mean = unweighted average of the two tables)
local paper copy (outside the repo): de_leva_1996.pdf sha256 685916ec701cd860ab861b1901e27a90f946199c221b23acedebfb9a7dc72811
